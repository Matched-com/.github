## What we're building

Matched is a two-sided hiring platform. Talent and companies each get an AI that reads profiles the way a great recruiter would, then surfaces the matches that actually fit.

## How matching works

```
  Build your profile  ─▶  AI reads skills, experience & resume  ─▶  Roles ranked by real fit  ─▶  Apply & connect
```

1. **Profile:** talent and companies describe skills, experience and needs.
2. **Score:** a skills-first matching engine scores every candidate–role pair. Skill overlap is required, not optional.
3. **Re-rank:** an AI model reviews the top matches and explains the fit.
4. **Fresh jobs:** new roles are imported and synced automatically, and closed listings are removed.
